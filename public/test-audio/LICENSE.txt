Download Test Playlist audio samples
====================================

The MP3 files in this directory are temporary fixtures for the mobile download
test playlist only. They are not recommendations, music catalogue content, or
part of any user's library.

Source: https://samplelib.com/

License note (from samplelib.com, checked 2026-11-21): "A completely free
library of test files available to download without licensing restrictions."
These files are bundled locally so the test provider does not depend on a
third-party host at download time.

Remove this directory and the temporary test-provider module together when the
download feature no longer needs test fixtures.
